Let's be honest: you've googled "JSON formatter" more times than you'd like to admit. And every time, you end up on some site with a modal that screams "Subscribe to Premium Pro Max!" just to format your clipboard contents.

Enough. Here's the shortlist of tools you should have bookmarked—five that actually work, stay out of your way, and handle the job in seconds.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

Because `JSON.parse()` errors in production are a special kind of pain. A good JSON formatter takes ugly one-liners and makes them readable, highlights syntax errors, and can even suggest fixes. Bonus points if it handles large files without lagging.

## 2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder

One of those things you need monthly, but when you need it, you *really* need it. Encoding API keys, embedding small images in CSS, passing binary data through JSON—Base64 comes up constantly. The best version is the one that just... works. In. Your. Browser.

## 3. Regex Tester & Visualizer

Writing a regex blind is a mug's game. A good regex tester shows you exactly which parts of your test string match, explains capture groups, and highlights what's going wrong when your pattern breaks. No more mystery character classes haunting you at 11 PM.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron syntax is famously unreadable. `*/5 9-17 * * 1-5` means "every 5 minutes during business hours on weekdays"—but good luck reading it at a glance. A cron parser converts the expression into human-readable text so you stop having to think about it.

## 5. Hash Generator (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256)

Verifying file integrity, hashing passwords for storage, checking checksums—sometimes you just need to run data through a hash function. Browser-based means nothing leaves your machine. Pure computation, pure privacy.

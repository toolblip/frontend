Every developer has a mental rolodex of tools they reach for daily. Here are five that deserve a permanent spot in your bookmarks bar — and the best part? They all run entirely in your browser.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

Debugging malformed JSON is one of the most frustrating parts of any API workflow. A good JSON formatter not only prettifies your data but validates it, highlights errors, and lets you collapse/expand nodes. Paste. Format. Fix. Done.

## 2. Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Base64 shows up everywhere — API auth headers, embedded images in CSS, JWT payloads. Having a reliable encoder/decoder that works offline in your browser means you never have to trust a random third-party site with your tokens or credentials again.

## 3. Regex Tester & Debugger

Writing a regex blind is a recipe for subtle bugs. A solid regex tester shows you exactly which parts of your test string match, highlights capture groups in different colors, and tells you when your pattern doesn't compile at all. Much better than staring at a wall of backslashes and hoping for the best.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron syntax is famously arcane. `*/5 9-17 * * 1-5` — wait, is that every five minutes, or every five minutes between 9 and 5 on weekdays? A cron parser that translates expressions into human-readable schedules saves a lot of head-scratching, especially when you're scheduling things across timezones.

## 5. Hash Generator (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, etc.)

Need to verify a file checksum, generate a password hash, or debug something related to cryptographic hashing? A browser-based hash generator handles MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and more — entirely client-side. No upload, no server, no risk.

## Honorable Mentions

URL encoder/decoder, UUID generator, color picker, JWT decoder — these come up constantly too. The beauty of browser-based tools is you can have a whole suite bookmarked and still not have used up the RAM of a single native app.

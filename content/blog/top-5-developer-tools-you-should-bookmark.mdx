Every developer has a set of utilities they reach for multiple times a day. Usually it's a messy mix of browser tabs, random websites with ads, and that one Stack Overflow answer with the regex you've copy-pasted 40 times.

Here's the thing: most of those tools can be replaced with fast, clean, browser-based alternatives that don't track you. These are the five you should have bookmarked right now.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

Staring at minified JSON is nobody's idea of a good time. A good JSON formatter takes that nightmare single-line blob and pretty-prints it with proper indentation — and flags syntax errors before you waste an hour debugging. Bonus points if it validates as you type.

## 2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder

Whether you're decoding an Authorization header, inspecting a JWT payload, or working with APIs that love Base64 encoding, you'll hit this constantly. A clean, local Base64 tool means no more `echo` commands or sketchy online decoders that might log your input.

## 3. Regex Tester

Writing a regex without testing it is a rookie move — and even experienced devs get burned. A live regex tester shows you exactly what matches, what groups capture, and highlights matches in real time as you type. It's the difference between "works in theory" and "works."

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron syntax is notoriously cryptic. `*/5 9-17 * * 1-5` — wait, does that mean every five minutes, or every minute on the fifth hour? A cron parser that humanizes the schedule and shows you the next N run times is invaluable, especially when configuring CI/CD pipelines or scheduled jobs.

## 5. Hash Generator

Generating MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256 hashes for files or strings comes up more often than you'd think — verifying downloads, checksumming config values, working with APIs that sign requests. Having a fast, offline-capable hash tool means you never have to trust a random website with your data.

## The Common Thread

All five of these tools share one trait: they're unsexy but unavoidable. Nobody writes a blog post about "the amazing JSON formatter that changed my life." But collectively, they save hours of friction every week.

**Try all five right now — no sign-up, no ads, all running locally in your browser.**

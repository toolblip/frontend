# Top 5 Developer Tools You Should Bookmark

Every developer has a workflow. Part of that workflow is a graveyard of browser tabs you've opened and closed a hundred times for the same small tasks. Let us save you the tab clutter — here are five tools that deserve permanent real estate in your bookmark bar.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

JSON breaks silently and catastrophically. A trailing comma, a missing quote, an errant `undefined` — your API call dies and you spend twenty minutes hunting the typo. A good JSON formatter validates as you paste, syntax-highlights the mess, and tells you exactly where things went wrong. Bookmark it. Your future self will thank you.

## 2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder

Still base64-encoding the hard way? Opening a random website, pasting your string, crossing your fingers it doesn't log your data somewhere questionable? Base64 encode and decode tools exist for a reason. Use one that runs locally, validate the output visually, and move on with your day.

## 3. Regex Tester

Writing a regex without testing it is like driving without brakes. A solid regex tester lets you input a pattern, throw test strings at it, and see matches highlighted in real-time. Bonus points if it explains what your pattern is actually doing — because let's be honest, that `([A-Z])(?=.*[a-z]).{8,}` you wrote at 11pm needs a second opinion.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron syntax is famously unreadable. `*/5 9-17 * * 1-5` — does that mean every 5 minutes during business hours on weekdays, or something else? A cron parser that reads human translations next to your expression is invaluable. Especially when you set up a cron job at 2am and need to be sure it fires at the right time.

## 5. Hash Generator

MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512 — sometimes you just need to hash something. Generating a hash locally means you're not accidentally transmitting sensitive data over the wire. Fast, local, done.

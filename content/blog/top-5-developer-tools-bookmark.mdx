Let's be real: as a developer, you probably Google the same small utilities over and over. "JSON formatter online", "Base64 decode", "cron expression generator". Save yourself the search — bookmark these five tools and never search for them again.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

Bad JSON is the bane of debugging. A proper JSON formatter does three things: pretty-prints your JSON with correct indentation, validates the syntax, and highlights where things went wrong. Bonus points if it can minify too — because sometimes you need to shrink a payload fast. Drop in messy JSON, get clean output. No more squinting at single-line errors.

## 2. Base64 Encoder / Decoder

Base64 comes up constantly — encoding images for data URIs, passing tokens, working with APIs that return base64-encoded content. A good Base64 tool handles both directions instantly, shows you the decoded result, and tells you if your input isn't valid base64. It's one of those tools that feels trivial until you need it and can't find a working one.

## 3. Regex Tester & Debugger

Writing regex is trial and error by nature. A solid regex tester shows you matches in real-time as you type, highlights capture groups in different colors, and explains what your pattern is actually doing. Writing `/^(?=.*[a-z])(?=.*[A-Z])(?=.*\d).{8,}$/` and trying to remember what that actually enforces? A visual tester fixes that instantly.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron syntax is notoriously opaque. `*/15 9-17 * * 1-5` — is that business hours on weekdays? (Yes, it is.) A cron parser takes the human out of the equation: input your expression, see the next N execution times laid out clearly. Useful for debugging scheduled jobs, configuring CI pipelines, or just confirming you set up that cron job correctly before it runs at 3am on a holiday.

## 5. Hash Generator

MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512 — hashing comes up in everything from password verification to checksums to blockchain. A fast hash generator that handles text and files, shows multiple algorithms at once, and copies results with one click is worth its weight in gold. Paste your data, get your hashes, move on.

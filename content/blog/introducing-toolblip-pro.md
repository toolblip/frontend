Today we're launching **Toolblip Pro** — our first paid plans after 6+ months of operating completely free.

Here's the short version: **Free tier stays free forever**. All existing users keep full access. Paid plans add more power, storage, and team features for users who want them.

---

## The Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Best for |
|------|---------|--------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | — | Using tools, no strings attached |
| **Starter** | $4.99 | $49.99/yr | Power users, no ads, 10MB files |
| **Ultra** | $19.99 | $199.99/yr | Heavy users, 100MB files, team seats |
| **Max** | $49.99 | $499.99/yr | Teams, 500MB files, priority support |

All yearly plans include **2 months free** — same as paying 10× the monthly price.

---

## Why Paid?

Running a fast, reliable tool platform has real costs: CDN bandwidth, server infrastructure, development time. We kept Toolblip free for as long as we could. Now paid plans let us keep improving it without selling your data or filling the site with ads.

The free tier isn't going anywhere. We believe the best way to earn paying users is to build something genuinely useful — not to trap people with a bait tier.

---

## What You Get With Pro

### No More Ads
Free users see respectful, relevant ads. Pro users see **zero ads** — clean tool interfaces, no distractions.

### Bigger File Processing
- **Free:** 5MB limit
- **Starter:** 10MB
- **Ultra:** 100MB
- **Max:** 500MB

### Cloud Storage
Pro plans include persistent cloud storage so your processed files are saved between sessions — not just a one-shot tool.

### Team Seats
Ultra includes **3 seats**, Max includes **10 seats**. Share one plan with your team instead of managing separate accounts.

### Priority Support
Max users get priority email support with guaranteed 24-hour response time.

---

## How Decoy Pricing Works

You'll notice Max is priced at $49.99/month — same as most individual SaaS plans. Here's the honest truth: **Max exists to make Ultra look cheap.**

Ultra is $19.99/month. That's 4× less than Max, for most of the features. Most people won't buy Max — they'll upgrade to Ultra and feel like they got a great deal. That's the decoy effect, and we're not hiding it.

We think Ultra is the right plan for most power users. Starter is for people who just want an ad-free experience. Max is for teams who need the extra seats and file size.

Every developer has a mental toolkit — the handful of utilities they reach for every day. Most of us have accumulated these slowly, often sticking with whatever we first found even when better options exist.

Here are five tools worth bookmarking right now.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator 🐍

Working with APIs? You're swimming in JSON. A good formatter that validates syntax, indents nicely, and highlights errors instantly will save you hours over a career. Bonus points if it handles large files without lagging.

## 2. Base64 Encoder / Decoder 🔤

Encoding API credentials, embedding small data payloads, working with token formats — Base64 comes up constantly. Having a tool that Encode ↔ Decode in one click, with proper UTF-8 handling (not the broken ASCII-only approach some tools use), is non-negotiable.

## 3. Regex Tester 🧩

Writing a regular expression is one thing. Testing it against real strings, understanding why it's not matching, iterating fast — that's where a dedicated regex tester earns its keep. Look for one that explains what your pattern actually matches, not just "yes" or "no."

## 4. Cron Expression Parser ⏰

Cron syntax is notoriously cryptic. `*/5 9-17 * * 1-5` — wait, is that every five minutes during business hours on weekdays, or...? A parser that reads human-readable times from cron expressions will either validate your existing expression or teach you what you actually wrote.

## 5. Hash Generator 🔐

MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512 — checking file integrity, comparing outputs, generating checksums for deployment pipelines. A good hash tool handles text and files, outputs all major formats, and does it instantly in-browser without uploading anything to a server.

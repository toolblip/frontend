Every time you upload an image to "optimize" it online, you're trusting someone else with your files. That company logo you're processing? That screenshot of an internal dashboard? That personal photo? They have it now — or at least had it long enough to do whatever they want with the copy.

There's a better way.

## Browser-Only Image Processing

Modern browsers have everything needed to process images without a server. Canvas API, the File API, and WebAssembly let you crop, resize, convert formats, and compress — all locally. The image loads in your browser, stays in your browser, and gets saved back to your machine. No server. No upload. No third party.

## Image Cropping & Resizing

Need to crop a hero image to the right aspect ratio or resize a batch of product photos? Load the image in a browser tool, drag to select your crop area, set your target dimensions, and download. All the computation happens in your tab. The original file is never modified until you explicitly save the result.

## Format Conversion

JPG to PNG. PNG to WebP. HEIC to JPG. (The last one is especially relevant if you've ever tried to use photos from an iPhone on anything else.) Browser-based format converters handle all of these without uploading anything. Choose your output format, adjust quality if applicable, and download.

## Compression Without Quality Loss You Can't See

Smart compression tools use the browser to re-encode your image at lower quality settings that still look great. You control the trade-off between file size and visual fidelity with a slider. Preview the result side-by-side before downloading. For most web use cases, you can cut file size by 50-80% without noticeable quality loss.

## Why This Matters

The cloud convenience of "upload and optimize" has real costs: upload time (especially on large files or slow connections), privacy exposure, and dependency on a service that might change, paywall, or shut down. Browser-based tools eliminate all three.

Here's a scenario that plays out constantly: you have an image that's too big, wrong format, or wrong dimensions — and every online "optimizer" wants you to upload it to their server first. That feels wrong. Your image might contain sensitive data, proprietary designs, or just photos you'd rather not send to a third party.

Good news: it doesn't have to be that way.

## Everything Happens Locally

Browser-based image tools can process images entirely on your device using the Canvas API and modern compression libraries. The image never leaves your machine. There's no server, no upload, no "your image has been received" confirmation. It just... works, right there in the tab.

This is a game changer for:

- **Privacy-sensitive work** — client photos, internal documents, wireframes
- **Large batches** — no upload latency, no bandwidth cost
- **Quick iterations** — adjust, preview, re-adjust in seconds

## Cropping Without Photoshop

Need to crop a hero image to a specific aspect ratio? A browser-based cropper lets you drag, resize, and set exact dimensions. No app to open, no project file to save. Export the result and you're done.

Resize tools let you set exact pixel dimensions or scale by percentage. Convert between formats — PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF — with a single click. Compress to reduce file size while keeping quality under control.

## Format Matters More Than You Think

A PNG screenshot might be 2 MB. Convert it to WebP and you're looking at 200 KB — with no visible quality loss. For photos, JPEG is still the king of compression. For graphics and screenshots, WebP or AVIF often look better at smaller sizes than PNG.

The right tool shows you the file size in real-time as you adjust quality settings. You can dial in exactly the right balance of quality vs. file size before exporting.

## No Upload, No Wait

Traditional image tools upload your file to a server, process it, then let you download the result. That means waiting for an upload, waiting for processing, and waiting for a download — all for something your browser could do faster locally.

With browser-based tools, the processing is instantaneous. You adjust, you preview, you download. Done.

There's a quiet revolution happening in developer tooling. The shift isn't dramatic — no viral launches, no breathless product hunt posts. It's the steady migration of utilities from installed apps to browser tabs. And honestly, it's about time.

## Privacy That Doesn't Require Trust

When you run a local app, you're trusting that app with your data. It has filesystem access, network access, god-knows-what-else in its dependencies. Browser-based tools? They're different. With client-side processing, your data never leaves your machine. No servers, no logs, no "we promise we don't look at your Base64 strings."

For tools that handle sensitive data — JSON formatters with API payloads, hash generators for password-related work, anything with credentials — this matters. A lot.

## Zero Install Friction

"How do I open this file?"  
"Install our desktop app."  
"I'm on Linux."  
"... Install our desktop app."

Sound familiar? Browser tools work everywhere. macOS, Windows, Linux, a chromebook from 2017, that old MacBook Air your colleague never formatted. As long as you have a browser, you have your tools. No installers, no updates to chase, no "sorry we don't support your OS version."

## Speed That Native Apps Can't Beat

Native apps need to boot up. Browser tabs are already there. When you're in the flow — rapidly iterating, debugging, processing data — every second counts. Opening a new browser tab is faster than launching an Electron app that swears it's "lightweight."

Plus, browser tools sync across machines. Your toolbelt follows you from your desktop to your laptop to that moment of inspiration at 2am on your tablet.

## The Real Win: Composable Workflows

Here's the underrated superpower: copy from one tab, paste into another. Chain browser tools together without any scripting, plugins, or integration theater. JSON output from an API? Paste it into your formatter. Hash a value? Paste the result into your converter. The browser is the glue.

It's a simple model, but simplicity at this level is genuinely powerful.

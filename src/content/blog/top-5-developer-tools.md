Let's be honest: most developers have a graveyard of browser tabs open at any given moment. Half of them are Stack Overflow answers. The other half are one-off tools you Google'd three months ago and never bookmarked.

Stop that cycle. Here are **5 tools you should have bookmarked right now**—all running in-browser, all private, all instant.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

Nothing kills a debugging session faster than staring at a wall of unformatted JSON. A good JSON formatter doesn't just pretty-print—it **validates** your data and shows you exactly where the syntax breaks. Paste, format, fix, done. No more "Unexpected token at position 42" mysteries.

## 2. Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Base64 shows up in more places than you'd expect: API authentication headers, image embedding in CSS/HTML, JWT payloads, and configuration files. Having a reliable encoder/decoder that's *just a tab away* saves you from that awkward moment when you try to decode something and get garbage.

## 3. Regex Tester

Writing regex is like speaking a secret dialect that only breaks when you need it most. A **regex tester** with live matching, explanation, and common pattern library means you write less, test faster, and actually understand what you wrote when you revisit it six months later.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron syntax looks like alphabet soup. `"0 0 */2 * *"` means every two hours—right? (Yes, but double-check anyway.) A cron parser that converts cryptic expressions into human-readable schedules and shows you the **next run times** is worth its weight in gold, especially when scheduling CI jobs or background tasks.

## 5. Hash Generator

Need an SHA-256 hash for a secret? Want to verify file integrity? Hash generators are one of those tools that seem niche until you desperately need one at 11 PM. MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512—all in one place, processed locally, no uploads.

Every developer has a mental folder of sites they visit when their code needs a quick fix. These five tools earn their place in that folder — fast, free, and processing everything locally so your data stays private.

**1. JSON Formatter & Validator**
Nothing kills momentum faster than staring at a wall of minified JSON trying to find the missing comma. A good JSON formatter not only prettifies your data but validates it on the fly. Paste ugly, get clean. Plus you can minify it again when you're done debugging.

**2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder**
Base64 shows up everywhere — API responses, embedded images, authentication tokens. Converting to/from Base64 by hand is error-prone and annoying. A quick encode/decode tool handles it instantly, and browser-based ones mean your strings never leave your machine.

**3. Regex Tester**
Writing a regex without testing it is like writing code without running it. A solid regex tester shows you exactly what matches, what doesn't, and highlights capture groups visually. It turns a painful trial-and-error process into something almost enjoyable.

**4. Cron Expression Parser**
Cron syntax is famously cryptic. `*/5 9-17 * * 1-5` — wait, is that every 5 minutes during business hours on weekdays, or something else? A cron parser that humanizes the expression and shows upcoming run times is worth its weight in gold when you're scheduling jobs.

**5. Hash Generator**
Need to MD5 a string? SHA-256 a file? Hash generators are indispensable for checking file integrity, storing passwords (with proper salting, of course), or just verifying that a download completed correctly. Browser-based generators work instantly without uploading anything.

Bookmark these five. You'll use at least one every week.

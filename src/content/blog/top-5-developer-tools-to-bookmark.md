Every developer has a mental toolbox—those handful of sites you open every single day. Here are five browser-based essentials that deserve a permanent spot in your bookmarks bar.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

When you're debugging an API response, staring at a wall of minified JSON is painful. A good JSON formatter does two things: pretty-prints your JSON and validates the syntax. If something's broken, it tells you exactly where.

Paste. Format. Done. No more `JSON.parse failed at line 47` with no context.

## 2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder

Base64 shows up in more places than you'd think—API authentication headers, data URLs in CSS, embedding images in HTML, JWT payloads. Having a reliable encoder/decoder handy means you stop doing weird mental gymnastics decoding `eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9`.

Bookmark it. You'll use it.

## 3. Regex Tester

Writing a regex without testing it is a gamble. A good regex tester shows you matches in real-time, highlights capture groups, and lets you toggle flags on and off. Some even explain what your pattern is doing in plain English.

If you've ever shipped a regex bug to production, you know why this matters.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron syntax is famously cryptic. `0 9 * * 1-5`—that's 9 AM, Monday through Friday. Easy to forget. A cron parser that converts expressions into human-readable schedules (and vice versa) saves you from counting asterisks.

Great for configuring CI/CD pipelines, scheduled jobs, and server cron tabs without second-guessing.

## 5. Hash Generator

MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512. Need to verify a download? Check a password? Generate an HMAC? A hash generator that runs entirely in your browser means you're not uploading sensitive data to random websites.

## The Common Thread

All five of these tools share the same qualities: they're fast, client-side (so nothing leaves your machine), and solve specific problems with zero friction. That's the bar for great developer tooling.

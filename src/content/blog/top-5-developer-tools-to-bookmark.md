Every developer has a mental Rolodex of sites they visit more than they'd like to admit. "Just one more time" turns into "that's in my top 5 most visited." Here are five tools that earn their bookmark bar real estate.

**1. JSON Formatter** — You know the pain. You get a wall of minified JSON dumped in Slack or an error log. You squint, scroll sideways, give up, paste it into a formatter, hit validate, realize there's a trailing comma on line 47, fix it, reformat, done. A good browser-based JSON formatter does all of this instantly and shows you exactly where the error is. No copy-paste to a third-party website with unknown privacy policies.

**2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder** — Whether you're dealing with API tokens, embedding small images in CSS, or debugging Authorization headers, Base64 comes up constantly. Having a fast, reliable encoder that works entirely in your browser means you never accidentally paste something sensitive into a server-side tool.

**3. Regex Tester** — Writing a regex blind is a great way to create something that matches everything or nothing. A good regex tester shows you live matches against your test string, explains your capture groups, and highlights what's going wrong. No reloads, no plugins, just instant feedback.

**4. Cron Expression Parser** — Cron syntax is famously unreadable at a glance. `*/15 9-17 * * 1-5` — is that business hours on weekdays? Every 15 minutes? A cron parser that humanizes the schedule (and shows you the next N run times) prevents 3 AM surprises from misconfigured jobs.

**5. Hash Generator** — Need to verify a file checksum, generate an HMAC, or just quickly hash a string? A local hash tool means the input never leaves your machine. No uploading to check an MD5 sum, no trusting a random site with your data.

Bookmark these five and you'll save yourself dozens of "wait, how do I decode this again?" moments.

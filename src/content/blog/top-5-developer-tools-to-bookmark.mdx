Every developer has a graveyard of one-off tools they installed for a single task and forgot about. Here's a better approach: bookmark these five, and keep them forever.

**1. JSON Formatter — [toolblip.com/tools/json](/tools/json)**

Messy API response? Pasting someone else's config and it looks like a wall of text? A good JSON formatter tidies it up with proper indentation, highlights keys and values, and can even validate syntax. Copy, paste, instant clarity. No more squinting at single-line JSON.

**2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder — [toolblip.com/tools/base64](/tools/base64)**

Handling Base64 shows up more often than you'd expect — encoding images for data URIs, passing tokens in headers, embedding small payloads. Having a fast browser-based encoder/decoder means you never have to remember command-line flags or open a separate app.

**3. Regex Tester — [toolblip.com/tools/regex](/tools/regex)**

Writing a regular expression without testing it is a gamble. A live regex tester lets you see matches as you type, explains what your pattern is actually doing, and helps debug why it's not matching what you expect. Essential for anything from input validation to log parsing.

**4. Cron Expression Parser — [toolblip.com/tools/cron](/tools/cron)**

Cron syntax is notoriously opaque. `0 8 * * 1-5` — wait, is that 8 AM Monday through Friday or every minute at 8 AM on the 1st through 5th? A cron parser that shows you the human-readable schedule and next N run times removes all the guesswork.

**5. Hash Generator — [toolblip.com/tools/hash](/tools/hash)**

Need to generate or verify MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, or other hashes? Browser-based hash generators let you paste text or upload files and get the hash instantly. Useful for verifying downloads, checksumming configs, or working with systems that store password hashes.

**Honorable mention:** URL encoder/decoder, UUID generator, color picker, and JWT decoder — because debugging usually involves at least three of these at once.

Every developer has a mental Rolodex of online tools they rely on daily. After a decade of building and shipping software, here are the five I keep bookmarked — and why each one earns its spot.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

Few things are more annoying than staring at a wall of minified JSON and trying to spot the missing comma. A good JSON formatter takes ugly, one-line JSON and turns it into something human-readable. Bonus points if it validates and shows you exactly where your parse error is. This is the tool I reach for before pasting JSON into a Slack message to make it readable.

## 2. Base64 Encoder / Decoder

Base64 shows up everywhere — API keys, token payloads, image data URLs. Whether you're encoding a string for an auth header or decoding a JWT to peek at its claims, a solid Base64 tool is indispensable. The best ones handle files too, not just text.

## 3. Regex Tester

Writing a regular expression without testing it is a recipe for surprise bugs in production. A regex tester lets you throw your pattern against sample text and see matches highlighted in real time. Most also explain what your pattern is doing, which is incredibly helpful when you're debugging someone else's cryptic creation.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron syntax is famously arcane. `*/15 9-17 * * 1-5` — wait, is that every 15 minutes during business hours on weekdays, or something else? A cron parser that humanizes your expression ("Every 15 minutes, 09:00–17:00, Monday through Friday") saves a lot of head-scratching and prevents scheduled jobs from running at the wrong times.

## 5. Hash Generator

Need an MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, or SHA-512 hash of a file or string? Whether you're verifying a download, storing a password safely, or checking file integrity, a hash generator is the tool you didn't know you needed until you need it. The browser-based kind keeps your data local, which matters when you're hashing anything sensitive.

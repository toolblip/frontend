Every developer has that tab they open ten times a day. You know the one — the slightly janky-looking site you use to decode Base64 or the regex tester that crashes on complex patterns. We've all been there. Here's the thing: those days are over. Let's talk about the five browser-based tools you should have bookmarked right now.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

Working with APIs and JSON is part of daily life. A good JSON formatter does more than pretty-print — it validates, catches syntax errors, and highlights exactly where your structure breaks. No more staring at a wall of minified text trying to find the missing comma.

## 2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder

Base64 shows up everywhere — from API authentication headers to embedding small images in CSS. Being able to encode and decode instantly, without sending your data to some random third-party site, is genuinely useful. Bonus points if it handles URL-safe Base64 variants.

## 3. Regex Tester & Debugger

Writing regex is easy. Writing *correct* regex is hard. A good regex tester shows you exactly what matches, what doesn't, and why. You can iterate fast without guessing and checking. No more deploying code that silently fails on valid input.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron is cryptic by design. A cron parser that shows you the next N execution times, explains each field in plain English, and validates your expression is worth its weight in gold — especially when you're scheduling something important and can't afford to get it wrong.

## 5. Hash Generator (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, etc.)

Hashing comes up constantly in security work, password checking, integrity verification. Having a fast, local hash generator means you never have to trust an online tool with your sensitive data. It's one of those tools you don't think about until you really need it.

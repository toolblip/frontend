Every developer has a mental toolbox — the small utilities they reach for dozens of times a week. The problem is these tools are often scattered across random websites, half of which are covered in ads or want you to sign up just to format JSON.

Here are five tools that deserve a permanent spot in your bookmarks bar.

## 1. JSON Formatter & Validator

JSON is everywhere, but reading raw JSON is painful. A good formatter does two things: pretty-prints your data so you can actually scan it, and validates it so you catch the trailing comma you missed. Bonus points if it handles large files without freezing your tab.

Toolblip's JSON formatter validates and pretty-prints in one shot. No signup, no ads, runs entirely in your browser.

## 2. Base64 Encoder/Decoder

Base64 shows up in authentication headers, URL-safe data encoding, image embedding, and about a dozen other places you don't expect. When you need to decode a Bearer token or encode a quick string, you don't want to write a throwaway script.

Bookmark a Base64 tool that handles both encode and decode directions. The best ones also handle URL-safe Base64 variant automatically.

## 3. Regex Tester

Writing regex without testing it is a special kind of optimism. A good regex tester shows you exactly what your pattern matches, highlights groups, and lets you iterate without guessing. Some even explain what your pattern is doing in plain English — useful when you're debugging someone else's cryptic creation.

## 4. Cron Expression Parser

Cron is notoriously hard to read. `*/5 9-17 * * 1-5` — is that business hours weekdays, or something else? A cron parser that humanizes your expressions is worth its weight in gold when scheduling jobs or debugging scheduled tasks.

## 5. Hash Generator

MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and more. You need to generate a hash when verifying downloads, checking file integrity, or working with APIs that sign requests. A multi-algorithm hash tool that works entirely in-browser means you never have to trust a third-party service with your data.

---

These five tools cover the small, frequent tasks that would otherwise interrupt whatever you're actually trying to build. Bookmark them once, use them forever.

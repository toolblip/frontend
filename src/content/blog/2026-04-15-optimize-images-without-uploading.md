Every time you upload an image to "optimize" it, you're trusting someone else with your file. That might be fine for cat pictures. It's probably not fine for screenshots of your dashboard, business documents, or anything sensitive. Here's the good news: you don't have to upload anything at all.

## The Browser Is More Powerful Than You Think

Modern browsers have APIs that let JavaScript read, manipulate, and save files entirely on your machine. The image never leaves your device. No server. No round-trip. No one else's servers holding a copy of your data. It's fast, private, and surprisingly capable.

## Crop Without Photoshop

Need to quickly trim the edges off a screenshot? A browser-based image cropper lets you select the exact region, preview it live, and download the result. No software to open, no export menus to navigate. Just load, crop, save.

## Resize Without Quality Loss

Resizing images for the web is a common task that usually means either installing software or uploading to a sketchy free tool. Browser-based resizers let you pick your exact dimensions or scale by percentage, choose your output format (JPEG, PNG, WebP), and download directly. The browser handles all the pixel math.

## Format Conversion — WebP, JPEG, PNG, AVIF

Different formats have different strengths. JPEG is great for photos, PNG for transparency, WebP for web performance. A browser-based format converter lets you switch between them without any server involvement. AVIF for next-gen compression? Sure. All happening locally.

## Why This Matters for Privacy

When you upload images to "free online tools," those files often sit on servers you don't control, sometimes indefinitely. For personal photos, work assets, or anything confidential, that's a real risk. Browser-based processing means your files stay on your machine, period.

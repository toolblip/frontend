Every designer has been there. You need to quickly crop a screenshot, convert a PNG to WebP, or shrink a photo before uploading to a service that inexplicably has a 2 MB limit. So you open Paint.net, or GIMP, or—worst case—you upload it to some random website that promises "fast, free processing."

That last option is a privacy problem. You're handing your image to a server you'll never see again. What happens to it? Who knows. But with browser-based image processing, you can have speed *and* privacy.

## Everything Happens Locally

Modern browsers can handle most image operations natively. Canvas API, WebAssembly codecs, and increasingly powerful built-in image manipulation mean you don't need a server to resize or convert an image. The pixels stay on your machine.

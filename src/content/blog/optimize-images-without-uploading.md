You need to resize an image. Crop it. Convert it from PNG to WebP. Your phone has a photo editor, but it saves to JPEG with heavy compression. Photoshop is overkill and slow. So you Google "image compressor online" and—oops—you just uploaded your design mockup to some random server you've never heard of.

That's a privacy problem disguised as a convenience. Here's a better way.

## Everything Happens Locally

Browser-based image tools can now do serious processing without ever sending your file to a server. **Your image stays on your device the entire time**—processed entirely in your browser using WebAssembly and Canvas APIs. There's no upload, no server-side processing, no copy sitting on someone else's infrastructure.

This matters for:
- **Work-in-progress designs** you don't want competitors to see
- **Screenshots with sensitive data** (credentials, names, internal info)
- **Large batches** where uploading to a server is slow and annoying

## Cropping Without Compromise

The crop tool in your browser can handle everything from social media aspect ratios (1:1, 16:9, 9:16) to custom dimensions. Drag, drop, adjust—**done in seconds**. No export dialogs, no "save a copy," no saving to the wrong folder.

## Format Conversion Done Right

Need a WebP for your website, a PNG with transparency for your design, or a smaller JPEG for email? A browser-based format converter handles all of these—letting you pick your output format, quality level, and dimensions before you download. All the control of desktop software, none of the bloat.

## Resize Without Losing Control

Resizing seems simple until you're trying to maintain proportions or hit exact pixel dimensions for a specific use case. A good browser tool lets you **set exact dimensions**, choose scaling behavior (stretch vs. fit vs. fill), and preview the result before you download.

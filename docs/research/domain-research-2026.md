## Domain Verification Checklist

Before registering any "likely available" domain, run through these steps:

1. **Namecheap bulk checker**: namecheap.com/domains/bulk-domain-search/
2. **Porkbun search**: porkbun.com (often cheaper than Namecheap for .com  -  ~$10.36/year)
3. **WHOIS lookup**: who.is or whois.com  -  check registration date and expiry
4. **Wayback Machine**: web.archive.org  -  check if the domain ever had a real business (trademark risk)
5. **Trademark search**: tmsearch.uspto.gov  -  search the brand name to check for active trademarks
6. **Google search**: `site:thedomain.com`  -  verify no indexed content exists

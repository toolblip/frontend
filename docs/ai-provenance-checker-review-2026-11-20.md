- Title, meta description, and the 5-item FAQ render server-side.

## Distinct purpose

Reads C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC DigitalSourceType, and AI generator metadata. This does not
overlap `exif-remover`, which removes personal metadata and does not interpret AI provenance.

## Honest copy

The page says what it cannot do. It does not verify C2PA signatures, it can't read pixel
watermarks such as SynthID, and a missing label does not prove a person made the image.
Unit tests: `lib/ai-provenance.test.ts`.
